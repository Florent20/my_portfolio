## The problem

Many parents in Cameroon struggle to have meaningful conversations with their children. Cultural gaps, busy schedules, and a lack of accessible resources leave families with few tools to talk about school, emotions, and daily life. Existing parenting apps are built for Western contexts — English-only, expensive, and disconnected from Cameroonian family realities.

## What I built

- **A bilingual web app** — French and English — so parents across Cameroon can use it regardless of their region.
- **Guided conversation prompts** — daily questions and activities designed around real Cameroonian family situations: school, faith, respect, and community.
- **A shared activity feed** — parents and children log moments together, creating a light history of their conversations.
- **A mobile-first interface** — most users access the app from phones on limited data plans, so every page is optimised for speed and small screens.
- **Accessible design** — large text, high-contrast colours, and clear navigation for users who are not used to complex apps.

## Result

- Built and shipped the full frontend from design to deployment.
- Reduced initial load time by keeping the bundle small and using Next.js static generation.
- Designed for low-bandwidth users — the app works on 3G connections.
- Bilingual interface serving both francophone and anglophone regions of Cameroon.
