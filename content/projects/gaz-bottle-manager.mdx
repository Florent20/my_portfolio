## The problem

Small gas-bottle distributors in Cameroon often track their inventory in paper notebooks or by memory. When a customer arrives, staff have to physically walk through the stock to count bottles — checking how many small, medium, and large bottles are available. This leads to slow service, mismatched counts, and lost or unaccounted-for bottles over time. There was no lightweight, browser-based tool to see stock at a glance.

## What I built

- **A live stock dashboard** — every bottle size (small, medium, large) displayed with its current quantity, updated in real time as stock changes.
- **Add, remove, and adjust stock** — simple controls to register incoming bottles and log outgoing sales, so the count always reflects reality.
- **A clean, minimal interface** — designed to be usable by staff with limited computer experience — big buttons, clear numbers, and no distracting menus.
- **Persistent local state** — inventory survives page reloads, so the app is usable without a backend or login.
- **Deployed on Vercel** — accessible from any phone or computer in the shop.

## Result

- Replaced manual counting with a single screen: staff can now see the full stock in under 2 seconds.
- Reduced bottlenecks at the counter — no more walking through the shop to check availability.
- Built entirely in React with no backend, keeping deployment and maintenance simple.
- Live at gazbottle-manage.vercel.app and usable from any device.
