## The problem

Finding a mentor is one of the highest-leverage moves for a developer's growth, but access to mentorship remains informal and uneven — especially in regions where networks are small and mentorship happens mostly through chance. Most people who want guidance have no structured way to find a mentor, request a session, or track their progress over time.

## What I built

- **A mentorship connection platform** — a full-stack web app where mentees can browse mentor profiles, send mentorship requests, and manage their ongoing relationships.
- **User authentication and roles** — separate experiences for mentors and mentees, with secure session handling on top of Express.js middleware.
- **A structured request workflow** — mentees send requests; mentors accept, decline, or propose a session. Requests move through clear states instead of dying in an inbox.
- **REST API backend** — built with Node.js and Express.js, with route-level validation and error handling.
- **Deployed on Vercel** — with a live production URL, so anyone can try the platform.

## Result

- Shipped the project end to end — backend API, authentication, mentor/mentee flows, and deployment.
- Live and publicly accessible at mentor-project-aptx.vercel.app.
- Designed the data model to support mentor profiles, mentee profiles, and request history — a foundation for future features like scheduling and messaging.
