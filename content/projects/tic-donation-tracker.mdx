## The problem

Small donation drives — church collections, school fundraisers, community events — often track contributions in notebooks or spreadsheets. When a donor asks "how much did I give last time?", or when the organizer needs the running total, someone has to manually scroll through rows and add them up. It's slow, error-prone, and easy to lose track of who's been thanked or followed up with.

## What I built

- **A donor registry** — every donor entered once, with their name and contact, so contributions are linked to a person rather than scribbled in a line.
- **A donation log** — record each donation with its amount and date, and see the full giving history per donor.
- **A live total** — the running sum of all donations, updated instantly as new entries are added, so organizers always know where they stand.
- **Searchable donor list** — find any donor in seconds instead of flipping through pages.
- **Mock data for demonstration** — the app ships with realistic sample donors and donations, so the interface can be explored immediately without setup.
- **Deployed on Vercel** — accessible from any browser, no install needed.

## Result

- Eliminated manual tallying — the total updates automatically with every donation.
- Made donor history instantly retrievable — one search instead of flipping through paper.
- Built entirely in React with mock data, keeping the project lightweight and easy to demo.
- Live at ticdonation.vercel.app and usable from any device.
